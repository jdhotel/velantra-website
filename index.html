Welcome to Vaigora

An Intimate Escape Crafted with Detail

Vaigora is Arakkonam's premier luxury boutique destination, marrying contemporary European design with elements of local craft and architectural heritage.

Conceived as an architectural haven, our hotel features just 18 carefully appointed guest rooms to maintain an atmosphere of exclusive privacy. We cater to the discerning business guest visiting the Arakkonam industrial hubs, as well as cultural travelers exploring the historic temples of Northern Tamil Nadu. Here, every detail is choregraphed—from the warm Russet Tan accents to the custom culinary creations in our bistro.

Gastronomy & Ambience

The Russet Bistro

A 48-seat boutique dining space serving handcrafted flavors that satisfy both global and traditional South Indian palates.

The Russet Bistro offers an intimate fine dining experience in Arakkonam. Our culinary team focuses on locally sourced produce, artisanal spices, and creative recipes. From rich morning filter coffee and fluffy idlis to masterfully executed continental main courses and tandoori classics, dining here is a sensory journey set against warm, textured interior walls and soft accent lighting.

Destination Guide

Explore Around Arakkonam

Vaigora is ideally positioned for business delegates and travelers wishing to explore key spiritual and cultural destinations in Tamil Nadu.

25 Mins Away

Tiruttani Murugan Temple

One of the six legendary sacred abodes (Arupadaiveedu) of Lord Murugan, perched beautifully atop a hill accessible by 365 stone steps or motorable road.

35 Mins Away

Sholinghur Narasimha Temple

An ancient and spiritually revered hill temple dedicated to Yoga Narasimha Swamy, drawing thousands of devotees to its tranquil, scenic peak.

45 Mins Away

Kanchipuram Silk Town

Known globally as the 'City of a Thousand Temples' and the home of exquisite handcrafted Kanchipuram silk sarees. A hub of ancient Pallava dynasty architecture.
